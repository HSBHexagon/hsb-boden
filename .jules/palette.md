## 2024-08-30 - Accessible Form Required Indicators and Focus States\n**Learning:** Relying solely on the HTML5 `required` attribute for form validation lacks visual affordance, potentially confusing users (especially those with cognitive or visual impairments) about which fields are mandatory before submission. Native focus outlines are also often stripped or insufficient, failing WCAG 2.1 SC 2.4.7 (Focus Visible).\n**Action:** Always append clear, semantic visual indicators (like `<span class="text-hsb-red" aria-hidden="true">*</span>` wrapped alongside the label text in a `<span>`) to required form fields. Additionally, apply explicit Tailwind `focus-visible` utility classes (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-hsb-red focus-visible:outline-none`) to all interactive form elements to ensure robust keyboard navigation support.
